About

The Barangay Resident Record Management System (RRMS) is a digital platform that helps barangay staff efficiently record, organize, and manage resident and household information.

It replaces manual record-keeping with a centralized and secure database, making data management faster and more reliable.

Designed to support barangay operations such as profiling, census, and reporting, RRMS ensures accurate information, quick access to records, and improved service delivery.

Why Use?

The Barangay Resident Record Management System (RRMS) helps barangays manage resident and household data easily and securely.

Efficient

Simplifies data recording, updating, and searching.

Accurate

Reduces errors and duplicate entries through a structured database.

Reliable

Generates reports instantly for faster decision-making and service delivery.

Secure

Protects sensitive resident information with admin-only access.

Features

Core capabilities designed for barangay resident management.

01 Resident Management

Add, update, view, or remove resident information quickly and accurately.

02 Household Profiling

Group residents by household and identify heads of families.

03 Search & Filter

Easily find residents by name, age, or address, with filters for voters, students, PWDs, etc.

04 Reports & Statistics

Automatically generate population data and printable reports.

05 Secure Access

Only authorized barangay staff can log in and manage sensitive information.

06 User-Friendly Interface

Simple, organized, and easy to navigate for faster barangay operations.

07 Data Export

Export datasets for reporting or transfer to other systems (CSV/PDF).

08 User Roles

Assign roles (admin, encoder, viewer) and manage permissions securely.

09 Analytics

Built-in statistics and charts to visualize population data and trends.
